Why I built it

My name is Igor. I have cerebral palsy and a first-group disability. My right hand works, my left barely does. I do everything on a computer with one hand.

I kept thinking: how do people like us play? Almost every game assumes a mouse and two hands — aim, turn, click. And Windows Mouse Keys simply don't work in games: games read the mouse at driver level and never see keyboard input.

I'm an engineer. Instead of giving up, I built a tool for myself — and for everyone in the same situation. That's how Game Mouse was born: it turns the keyboard into a full mouse and works where ordinary solutions fail.

I made it to play myself. And I share it with everyone who needs it — free. Because access to games and work is not a luxury; it's a normal part of life.

How it works

Four steps and the mouse is no longer needed.

Step 1Download and install Game Mouse.
Step 2Turn on NumLock — keys work on the numeric pad.
Step 3Move with 8/4/6/2, click with 5 and /, drag with 0 and .
Step 4For games enable “Virtual mouse” (F10) and play.

A simple window: the numeric pad with highlights, settings by section, status on the right.

FAQ

Does it work in games?

Yes. Windows Mouse Keys are ignored by games, so Game Mouse uses a kernel-level virtual mouse (Interception driver) — the game sees real movement and clicks.

Do I need a mouse at all?

No. The mouse can be disconnected — everything is done from the keyboard.

Is internet required?

No, it works offline.

Is it safe?

The app collects and sends no data. The Interception driver is open and used by professional tools.

How much does it cost?

Free for personal use. Donations are welcome but optional.
